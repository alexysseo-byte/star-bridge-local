Privacy Policy — Star Bridge Local

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Who uses it

Star Bridge Local is a personal, single-user desktop application. It is not distributed to or used by anyone other than its owner.

Google data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app requests the read-only Google Sheets scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.readonly) and reads the rows of one spreadsheet chosen by the owner. It does not access Gmail, Drive files, contacts, or any other Google data, and it never modifies Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Spreadsheet rows are copied into a local database on the owner's own computer, solely to display work-status cards to the owner.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local app data folder.
